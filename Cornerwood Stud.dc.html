About

A family-run stud in East Sussex

Two decades of producing, sourcing and selling sport horses for clients across the UK, Europe and Ireland.

Cornerwood Stud & Sport Horses is run by Harriet, who specialises in producing and selling handpicked horses for their ability and temperament. Cornerwood also acts as an agent for some of the most reputable breeders and private owners in Europe and Ireland, helping clients find the right horse for their goals.

As well as her own horses for sale, Harriet takes horses on a sales livery basis — handling advertising, enquiries and viewings, with owners as involved as they'd like to be.

Cornerwood has worked hard to build a name it's proud of, with many satisfied clients who return and recommend the yard to others.

Location & Setting

Set in an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty

Cornerwood Stud lies in the historic town of Battle, within an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The yard is reached directly from the A21 and is an hour from Gatwick and around an hour and a half from London.

It sits behind the grounds of 1066 Battle Abbey on 30 acres of well kept grazing. The property includes 5 acres of ancient woodland bordering the RSPB's Fore Wood, where hundreds of acres of woodland and a network of good tracks make for safe and peaceful hacking.

Beyond the woods there are quiet country lanes with open fields and bridleways to explore.

Services

Horse Sales

Sourcing and selling quality, handpicked horses — and representing trusted breeders and private owners across Europe and Ireland.

We pick horses on how they go and how they behave, not on what they cost. Every horse is ridden by us before it's advertised, so we can tell you honestly what it's like. Viewings are by appointment, so you get the time to try the horse properly.

When we're acting as agent, we're working for the breeder or private seller, and our job is to put the right buyer and the right horse together. If a horse isn't right for you, we'll say so.

  • Handpicked horses assessed for ability and temperament
  • Agency representation for breeders across Europe & Ireland
  • Viewings, trials and vettings arranged for serious buyers
  • Honest guidance on suitability, not just a sale
Services

Sales Livery

Taking horses on a sales livery basis — advertising, enquiries and viewings handled for you, with as much or as little involvement as you like.

We take a small number of horses on sales livery at any one time, so every horse gets proper attention rather than being lost in a big yard. Owners stay as involved as they'd like to be — from hands-off through to regular updates.

Services

Producing & Schooling

Bringing young or green horses on with correct, sympathetic schooling to bring out their best.

Whether a horse is just backed or already competing, we take the time to build confidence and correct the basics before asking for more — no shortcuts, no rushing.

Horses are ridden and assessed regularly throughout their time with us, so progress is honest and owners always know exactly where they stand.

  • Starting young or green horses from scratch
  • Correcting confidence or schooling issues
  • Flatwork, poles and jumping fundamentals
  • Regular ridden assessment and feedback for owners
Services

Breeding & Stud Services

Stallions standing at stud each season, with a small number of spaces for outside mares.

We keep a small, carefully chosen stallion line-up each season rather than a long list — quality over quantity, with limited bookings so every mare gets proper attention.

  • A small, carefully chosen stallion line-up each season
  • Limited bookings for outside mares
  • Guidance on suitability and covering arrangements
  • Full breeding details available on enquiry
