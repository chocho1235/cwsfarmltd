About

A family-run stud in East Sussex

Two decades of producing, sourcing and selling sport horses for clients across the UK, Europe and Ireland.

Cornerwood Stud & Sport Horses is run by Harriet, who specialises in producing and selling handpicked horses for their ability and temperament. Cornerwood also acts as an agent for some of the most reputable breeders and private owners in Europe and Ireland, helping clients find the right horse for their goals.

As well as her own horses for sale, Harriet takes horses on a sales livery basis, handling advertising, enquiries and viewings, with owners as involved as they'd like to be.

Cornerwood has worked hard to build a name it's proud of, with many satisfied clients who return and recommend the yard to others.

Selling livery

A selling livery is a horse that is not the property of Cornerwood Stud. This will be a horse sold on behalf of a client and this will be stated on the advert of the horse.

If a horse gets injured and requires veterinary treatment the client must pay. We will list all horses on our social media platforms along with our website (we have a huge following of over 10k) free of charge. Any other advertising, e.g. HorseQuest, will be charged to the owner.

If for whatever reason the horse proves to be unsuitable, this will not reside with Cornerwood Stud but must be taken up with the previous owner. However, Cornerwood Stud will hold the funds for one week before transferring to the previous owner.

Distance selling and your right to cancel

All transport costs for horses sold under distance selling rules will be down to the buyer, including transport to and from Cornerwood Stud.

When this applies. If you are a consumer and you buy a horse from us without meeting us in person at the yard before the contract is made (for example entirely by telephone, email, WhatsApp, video call or through our website), this is a distance contract and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 apply. This section applies to consumers only and does not apply to trade to trade sales.

Your right to cancel. You have the right to cancel a distance contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The cancellation period expires 14 days after the day on which you, or a person you nominate (other than the carrier), take physical possession of the horse.

How to cancel. To exercise the right to cancel you must tell us of your decision by a clear statement, for example by email to info@cornerwoodstud.co.uk or by telephone on +44 7974 941333 or +44 1424 772484. We may withhold the refund until we have received the horse back, or until you supply evidence that you have returned it, whichever is earlier. We will refund using the same method of payment you used for the purchase.

Returning the horse. You must return the horse to Cornerwood Stud without undue delay, and in any event within 14 days of telling us that you cancel. Location & Setting

Set in an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty

Cornerwood Stud lies in the historic town of Battle, within an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The yard is reached directly from the A21 and is an hour from Gatwick and around an hour and a half from London.

It sits behind the grounds of 1066 Battle Abbey on 30 acres of well kept grazing. The property includes 5 acres of ancient woodland bordering the RSPB's Fore Wood, where hundreds of acres of woodland and a network of good tracks make for safe and peaceful hacking.

Beyond the woods there are quiet country lanes with open fields and bridleways to explore.

Producing & Schooling
